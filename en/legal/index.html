Accessibility

I want the site to be usable by everyone. That is why:

  • all the content of the 3D workshop is also available as a text version and on the classic site, usable with keyboard and screen readers;
  • in the workshop the interactive points are buttons reachable with the Tab key, Esc closes the panels and the arrow keys move through the guided tour;
  • images have alternative text, colours keep good contrast and the site reduces animations when "reduce motion" is on in your device settings;
  • music and sounds turn off with a single button, and the site works without audio.

If something is hard to use, write to crapanzano.work@gmail.com: I will fix it and send you the content in another format in the meantime.
