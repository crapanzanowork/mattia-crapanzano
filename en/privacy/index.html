2. What data and why

Technical browsing data

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Like any web server, it records technical connection data such as your IP address and browser type to run the site and protect it from abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Anonymous statistics

To understand how many people visit and what they find useful, I use Umami Cloud (Umami Software, Inc.), with data stored in the European Union. Umami uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device and cannot identify visitors: it counts, in aggregate, page views, country, device type, referring site and a few actions on the site (for example opening a panel in the workshop, downloading a résumé, clicking a contact link). It does not record what you type in the search box. Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you contact me

If you email me, call me or send a message, I use the details you give me (name, contact details, message) only to reply and, if we start working together, to manage the job (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and meet tax and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). The "Message me" button opens WhatsApp, a Meta service: WhatsApp's privacy policy also applies.

Third-party content

  • YouTube and Vimeo videos: thumbnails are stored on this site and videos load only when you press play, YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and Vimeo with "do not track" on. From then on Google or Vimeo receive browsing data under their own policies.

  • External links (LinkedIn, Instagram, client websites): when you open them you leave this site and their policies apply.
  • Fonts are hosted on this site and are not requested from Google.
