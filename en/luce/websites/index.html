05 — Maintenance

Rules change. Your site has to keep up.

A site left alone gets old. Privacy and cookie rules change, platforms change, browsers too. And if the site isn’t compliant, the risk falls on whoever owns it.

  1. Privacy · GDPR

    The privacy notice must tell the truth

    It must describe the data the site really collects, and change when the site changes: a new form, a statistic, an advertising pixel.

  2. Cookies · since 2022

    The banner has precise rules

    The Italian Data Protection Authority’s guidelines apply: closing the banner must mean refusing, and scrolling the page doesn’t count as consent.

  3. Accessibility · since 28/06/2025

    Usable by everyone

    The European Accessibility Act requires online shops and many digital services to be usable by people with disabilities too.

  4. VAT number

    On the home page

    In Italy, anyone with a VAT number must show it on the home page of their website.

With maintenance, I take care of it
  • I update privacy, cookie and legal pages when the rules change
  • I update the platform and security, and keep backups
  • I check that the site works and loads fast
  • I change text, photos and opening hours when you ask

09 — Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on pages, languages and features. First we talk on a video call, then I send you a written quote.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You do. The domain is registered to you, and I set up a dedicated email through which everything I build for you online goes: site, profiles and campaigns.

Can I sell online from the site?

Yes: catalogue, cart, online payments and shipping. I’ll recommend the right solution for how many products you have and how you ship.

Can I edit it myself?

On Wix, yes, from a simple dashboard. On a custom site you ask me for changes and I make them.

I already have a website. Can you look after it?

Yes, if it’s on Wix: first I check it and make it compliant, then I keep it updated with maintenance. If it’s on another platform, we decide together whether to rebuild it on Wix or custom.

Do I really need a cookie banner?

Only if the site uses non-technical cookies, for example advertising or profiling statistics. On a custom site it can often be avoided, like on this one.

Got a site to build or fix?

First a video call, then a tailored quote. I reply myself, usually the same day.
