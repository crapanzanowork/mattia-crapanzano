06 — Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can you send me a quote?

Yes, after a video call. Without understanding what you need, it would be a random number.

How many changes can I ask for?

Two rounds of revisions are included. Others are agreed separately.

In how many formats do you deliver the video?

The ones written in the quote, for example 9:16 for social media and 16:9 for YouTube. Other formats can be added.

How long does it take?

It depends on the job. We write the timing in the quote, before starting.

Do you also shoot with a drone?

Yes, with the EU EASA A1/A3 certificate.

Got a story to tell?

An event, a video or photos: first a video call, then a tailored quote.
